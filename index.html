Our Story

More Than BBQ.
It's About Bringing People Together.

Not Yo Mama's BBQ is a Black-owned and LGBT-owned catering company founded by Anthony Schmidt, Founder & Pitmaster Chef. Our mission is simple: bring people together through bold barbecue, soulful comfort food and inclusive hospitality.

We're building a business rooted in family, culture, community and the belief that everybody deserves a place at the table. From weddings and family celebrations to corporate lunches and community events, we're here to serve memorable food with personality.
