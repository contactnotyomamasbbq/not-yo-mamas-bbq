Our Story

More Than BBQ.
It's About Bringing People Together.

Not Yo Mama's BBQ is a Black-owned and LGBT-owned catering company founded by Anthony Schmidt, Founder & Pitmaster Chef. Our mission is to bring people together through bold BBQ, soulful comfort food and inclusive hospitality.

We serve weddings, private events, corporate functions, breweries and community gatherings through drop-off buffet catering, staffed full-service catering and corporate boxed lunches.

Built for West Michigan Events

  • Weddings & receptions
  • Graduation parties
  • Corporate lunches & employee events
  • Brewery partnerships & pop-ups
  • Community festivals & neighborhood events
